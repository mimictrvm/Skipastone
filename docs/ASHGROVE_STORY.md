# Ashgrove House: The Story

**Status:** story bible, v1 (4 Oct 2026). It sits under the [creative direction](ASHGROVE_DIRECTION.md): the direction says how the game plays, this says what happens. Story 1 is built and playable in [`ashgrove/`](../ashgrove/README.md). Stories 2–5 are written here so the map, the notes and the monsters can be built towards them.

---

## The premise

Cornelius Ashgrove spent forty years buying haunted objects. Three months ago he vanished from his cliff-top house. The auction house that's selling his estate sent a crew to catalogue the collection, and the crew never came back. You're the second crew: retrievers, armed because of what happened to the first. You came for one night's work. It takes three nights, and by the end you understand why the house needed you.

## The secret

This is for the team. Players uncover it over five stories.

1. **The Banshee came with the Ashgrove family, not with any object.** She keens before an Ashgrove dies. In 1979 she keened for Cornelius. That night his brother Edmund died in his place, and Cornelius found out why: a death in the house can answer her keen.
2. **Cornelius made a bargain to keep it that way.** The Creditor (the Demon) agreed that every keen for Cornelius would be answered by some other death in the house, for as long as he paid. The payment was the collection. Every haunted object he bought was another bound thing that could die, or kill, under his roof. The collection is his life insurance. This is why the core rule works: *when she keens, something nearby will die. If nothing else dies, you will.*
3. **She took her comb back from him.** Lot 077, "missing", is the banshee's silver comb, taken from the family centuries ago. Cornelius kept it so she couldn't settle. He has it with him.
4. **Cornelius isn't dead.** He's sealed in the strongroom under the hall. He's been kept alive three months without food or water by the terms of the bargain, waiting for the next death that will pay for him.
5. **The auction house knows.** Pellam & Sloane are the Creditor's agents. They don't send crews to catalogue the collection. They send crews to keep it paid.
6. **The sealed box was opened on purpose.** Lot 001 holds the soul of Isaac Lerner, the cabinet-maker who built the box and whom Cornelius ruined to get it. Lerner died owed. The first crew opened the box because Pellam & Sloane told them to. Lerner's soul took Wren, the one first-crew member who lived. What Lerner wants isn't a body. It's his name back and his debt paid.

Every story reveals one more of these.

## Rules of the world

These hold in every story. New stories add to them and never break them.

- **Every entity is bound to an object.** Killing one banishes it back into its object for a while. Securing the object ends it.
- **The Banshee never chases and is always heard first.** When she keens, something has to die, or you get out, or you keep the wake custom (stop the clock, cover the mirror, open the window).
- **Thresholds hold:** a sanctuary's doorway, a lit hearth, the open air.
- **Each entity has one rule, written in Cornelius's catalogue.** Its counter comes from its own folklore (gold for the Dullahan, light for the Lamp-shy, and so on).
- **The house remembers.** Bodies stay where they fell, kept customs stay kept, and secured crates stay shut.
- **The storm builds over a story, and silence means a keen.**

## Story 1: The Second Crew *(built)*

**Night 1. Monsters: the Dullahan, the Lamp-shy (stand-in), the Banshee. The sealed box. Map: the drive, the hall, the gallery, the servants' wing, the cellar vault.**

| Chapter | What happens | What it reveals |
|---|---|---|
| I. The Second Crew | You arrive at the van. The hall is the first crew's camp: a generator, the manifest, catalogue cards, Okafor's memo | The job; the first crew's fate; the rules written down |
| II. The Portrait Gallery | The Dullahan studies faces. You shoot the head from his hands and secure the spine whip. A keen far off | Entities can be killed but not ended; only crating ends them |
| III. The Servants' Wing | The bell board. The Banshee grieving over Okafor; Wren's memo, readable only while she grieves. The first keen. You secure the lantern, and the hall's power dies | **She doesn't hunt; she announces.** "She was wailing before it happened." |
| IV. The Cellar Vault | The sealed box is open and empty. The ledger: "it is already wearing someone". She keens with nothing to kill. You escape to the van | **Secret 6, half-seen:** someone left the house wearing something |

**Ending.** In the rain at the van, she's still keening. It wasn't about you. Then the first crew's dead van **flashes its headlights once.**

**Seeds planted for later:**
- The study door in the hall: *"C.A. PRIVATE. The lock is newer than the door."* (Story 5)
- The roped-off stair (Stories 3–4).
- The chained cliff stair (Story 2).
- The lit attic window that goes dark sometimes (Story 4).
- The open grave in the family plot, with the sexton's chit: *"Do not fill it until she stops."* (Secrets 1 and 4)
- Lot 077's empty cushion (Secret 3).

## Story 2: Low Water

**Night 2, early. Monster: the Siren (Greek). Lot 152: a ship's figurehead from a wreck off Kythira. Map: the cliff stair, the boathouse, the sea cave under the house. New mechanic: the tide.**

**Rule:** *its song pulls you towards it. Covering your ears stops the pull, and stops you aiming.*
- Every encounter is a choice: hear it and aim, or go deaf and be safe.
- Co-op answer: one player covers their ears and guides the others; another listens and shoots.
- It's killed in the water at the cave mouth, where it surfaces to sing. Its death leaves a slick of light on the water.

**Tide:** the cave floods and drains on a cycle you can see and hear, not one on a timer. Low water opens the deep cave. High water drowns it. This is the time-gated space the research recommended.

| Chapter | What happens | What it reveals |
|---|---|---|
| I. The Radio | The van's radio crackles: Sloane from Pellam & Sloane, warm and apologetic, offers double pay to finish. Then Wren's voice on a second channel: *"I'm under the house. The water's coming."* | The auction house is still involved. Wren is alive |
| II. The Cliff Stair | The chain on the cliff gate has been cut from the inside. Wet stone, the stair down, the boathouse | Someone came up this way |
| III. The Boathouse | The figurehead is gone from its cradle. Its song rises with the tide. Rescue Wren from the cave at low water | The Siren's rule, the tide's rule |
| IV. Low Water | Secure the figurehead in the deep cave before the tide turns. Get Wren up the stair | |

**The tell.** On the way back up, the Banshee appears on the stair and kneels by Wren, mourning them while they're still alive. Nobody else is close enough to see it. Mourning the living is something she has never done.

**Ending.** Wren won't get in the van: *"I left something in the study."* Sloane, on the radio, agrees that the crew should stay the night.

**Map needs:** the cliff stair (behind `CliffStairGate`), a boathouse at sea level, a two-level sea cave, and a tide-water part that rises and falls.

## Story 3: The Guest Wing

**Night 2, late. Monster: the Aswang (Filipino). Lot 233: a carved narra-wood chest from a Visayan estate. Map: the upper floor, east (guest bedrooms over the servants' wing). New mechanic: sound you can't trust.**

**Rule:** *its call is loud when it's far away and quiet when it's close.* This comes from tik-tik folklore, and it inverts the habit every Roblox horror player has (footsteps getting louder means danger).
- It hunts by the scent of blood: it goes for whoever has taken the most hits.
- Salt and ginger (both found in the house) keep it off a doorway, a temporary threshold.
- It's killed when it drops from the ceiling to feed. Its death leaves the smell, and the next hour's other entities are drawn to that room.

**Care:** the aswang comes from the folklore of the Visayas and other regions of the Philippines. Build it from that folklore and its regional variants, not from a generic vampire. The catalogue card names where Cornelius bought it and from whom. No caricature.

| Chapter | What happens | What it reveals |
|---|---|---|
| I. Upstairs | The rotten stair is repaired: the first crew's planks, laid by Wren that afternoon. Wren knows the way through rooms none of you have seen | Wren knows too much |
| II. The Guest Rooms | Cornelius's guests' letters. Sloane's letters among them: shipping notes, "replacement crew" | **Secret 5:** the auction house sends crews to die |
| III. Tik-tik | Hunted by an entity whose sound lies. Wren's voice calling from rooms Wren isn't in | |
| IV. The Diary | Secure the chest. In Cornelius's dressing room, his diary: *"Every night something must die in this house, or I do."* | **Secret 2:** the bargain. The Banshee's rule is a contract's clock |

**Ending.** Sloane's voice on the radio again, no longer warm: *"Keep the house fed and you'll be paid. Leave, and the next crew will feed it with you."* The road's gone under the storm. You're staying.

**Map needs:** the upper floor east (guest bedrooms, a dressing room, a linen room, a back stair), reached by the repaired grand stair. The `UpperStair` door unlocks.

## Story 4: The Long Sleep

**Night 3. Monster: the Nightmare, the Mare (Germanic/Norse). Lot 301: a carved bed-board from a Norwegian farmhouse. Map: the upper floor, west (the nursery and the master bedroom over the hall; the lit attic window). New mechanic: rest.**

**Rule:** *it only moves while you're still.*
- This punishes hiding and waiting, the two strongest Roblox horror reflexes.
- Players have to rest: standing still in a sanctuary slowly recovers hits and battery. Rest is also when the Mare comes. Co-op answer: watch shifts. One player stays moving and awake while the others rest.
- It's killed by the one keeping watch, at the moment it crouches on a sleeper's chest. Its death leaves the sleeper waking with a hit healed.

| Chapter | What happens | What it reveals |
|---|---|---|
| I. Shifts | The storm has the house. The crew has to rest in shifts in the nursery, the last room with working light | |
| II. Dreams | Each time a player rests, they dream: 1979, this house, Edmund in the nursery, the Banshee keening for Cornelius and Edmund dying instead | **Secret 1:** she's the family's, and a death in the house answers her |
| III. The Master Bedroom | Secure the bed-board. Cornelius's key to the study is under his pillow | |
| IV. Isaac | Wren sleepwalks to the study door and speaks in an older voice: *"My name is Isaac Lerner. He has my name in his book."* The lit attic window was Wren's: the room where the box was opened | **Secret 6:** who's inside Wren, and what he wants |

**Ending.** The key turns, and the study is open.

**Map needs:** the upper floor west (nursery, master bedroom, the attic room behind the lit window). Dream versions of the nursery: the same room in 1979, re-dressed and lit differently.

## Story 5: The Bargain

**Night 3, to dawn. Monster: the Creditor (the Demon). Lot 000, unlisted: the contract ledger. Map: Cornelius's study (north of the hall, behind `StudyDoor`) and the strongroom beneath the hall. New mechanic: offers.**

**Rule:** *it doesn't hunt. It bargains.* It's the threat that judges instead of hunting, the one the research found almost unused.
- When the Banshee keens, the Creditor offers to answer it for you: it will kill an entity, or take someone else. Every offer has a price:
  - a crewmate's hits;
  - your torch;
  - a secured object let out of its crate;
  - your name in the ledger, which matters for the ending.
- It can't be shot. It's ended by the ledger.

**Care:** the Creditor is a creature of debt and contract, not of any religion. No real-world religious symbols, scripture or ritual, as the brief requires. Its look comes from ledgers, wax seals, coin and bone: the horned model plus the props of a debt-collector.

| Chapter | What happens | What it reveals |
|---|---|---|
| I. The Study | Cornelius's study: the collection's true ledger. Every lot is listed as "collateral" | The whole collection was payment |
| II. Collateral | The Creditor arrives to collect, because nothing died last night. Every bargain you take or refuse is recorded in the ledger | The rule as a mechanic |
| III. The Strongroom | Down beneath the hall: Cornelius, alive, three months without food, the silver comb in his fist | **Secrets 3 and 4** |
| IV. Dawn | She keens one last time. It's for Cornelius, as it always was. The choice | |

**Endings.** The players' choices stack.

| Choice | What you do | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| **Let her finish** | Take the comb from Cornelius and lay it where she mourns | Her keen is answered at last. Cornelius dies. The bargain has nothing left to protect; the Creditor closes the ledger and goes. Every bound entity in the collection is released |
| **Restitution** | Find Isaac Lerner's name in the ledger and strike out his debt | Lerner leaves Wren. Wren lives |
| **Take the contract** | Sign the ledger when the Creditor offers | Cornelius lives and walks out into the rain. The Banshee turns to face the player who signed. *Now she keens for you.* This is the sequel ending |

The **best ending** is Let her finish plus Restitution, with no bargains taken. The game shouldn't announce it; players find it.

**Epilogue** (any ending): the van at dawn, the house behind you, the radio on the dashboard. Sloane, very calmly: *"There'll be another collection."*

**Map needs:** the study (north of the hall), a stair down under the grand stair, and the strongroom (a vault-like room beneath the hall, separate from the cellar).
